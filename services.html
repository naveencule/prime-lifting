Our Services

We Provide Quality Structural Services

We provide reliable house lifting and foundation solutions designed to improve safety, elevation, and long-term structural strength. Our team follows proven engineering practices to ensure precise execution and dependable results. With a focus on quality workmanship and careful planning, we deliver services that meet structural requirements and customer expectations.

Site Check

Engineers inspect the building, foundation, soil condition, and surroundings. Load calculations and feasibility checks are done to plan a safe lifting method.

Preparation Work

The house is prepared by disconnecting utilities and strengthening weak areas. Hydraulic jacks, steel beams, and temporary supports are installed to distribute the load evenly.

House Lifting

Using synchronized hydraulic jacks, the house is lifted slowly and uniformly to the required height. Continuous monitoring ensures balance and structural safety during the process.

New Foundation

A new raised foundation or columns are constructed at the lifted level. Once cured, the house is securely placed, aligned, and all connections are restored

01. Residential House Lifting

We provide safe and controlled house lifting services for residential buildings using advanced hydraulic systems and proven engineering techniques. Each structure is carefully inspected and assessed to ensure proper balance, load distribution, and overall stability throughout the lifting process. Our team follows a planned, step-by-step approach to protect walls, foundations, and structural components while minimizing stress and risk. By maintaining precision and continuous monitoring, we achieve the required elevation safely, ensuring long-term durability, structural integrity, and complete customer satisfaction.

02. Foundation Elevation Work

Our foundation elevation services are designed to strengthen existing structures and significantly improve ground clearance, effectively mitigating flood risks and soil-related vulnerabilities . Through rigorous planning and engineered execution, we ensure maximum durability and long-term stability. This precise lifting process helps homes withstand environmental challenges and rising water levels while maintaining uncompromised structural integrity.

03. Flood Protection Lifting

We specialize in lifting houses to reduce flood risks and protect structures from water-related damage. By elevating buildings above identified flood levels, we help safeguard homes from seasonal flooding and prolonged water exposure. Our process is carefully planned based on site conditions, soil type, and structural requirements to ensure effective results. Using practical and reliable methods suited to local environmental conditions, we deliver long-lasting solutions that enhance safety, durability, and peace of mind for homeowners.

04. Structural Reinforcement Services

We offer comprehensive structural reinforcement services during and after the house lifting process to ensure enhanced strength, safety, and long-term stability. This includes the installation of high-quality support beams, precise load balancing, and detailed alignment checks at every stage of the work. Our team carefully monitors structural performance to prevent stress, cracks, or imbalance. Through proper reinforcement and finishing, we ensure the structure remains secure, durable, and capable of withstanding environmental and load conditions for many years to come.
